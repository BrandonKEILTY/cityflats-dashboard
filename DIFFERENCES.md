# Parsers vs the 2026-10-02 feed

The tables further down are generated by `python -m parsers.diff_report` and list every place the parsers and the feed disagree. Brandon ruled on the ten points raised; the rulings are recorded here and built into the parsers.

## Dates do not line up

The four fixture zips are Entrata exports from 2026-09-30 evening (Grove 18:13, Faculty47 17:56) and 2026-10-01 11:51 EDT. `feeds/2026-10-02.json` was built from the 2026-10-02 01:00 MDT emails, about a day newer. Most remaining differences below are overnight movement (suites changing status, work orders aging a day, payments coming in). Use the export as it is.
