# Parsers vs the 2026-10-02 feed: differences for Brandon to rule on

Nothing below has been "fixed" on either side. The tables further down are generated by `python -m parsers.diff_report`.

## Read this first: the dates do not line up

- The four zips are Entrata exports run on **2026-09-30 evening** (Grove 18:13, Faculty47 17:56) and **2026-10-01 11:51 EDT** (both properties, data as of that time).
- `feeds/2026-10-02.json` was built from the **2026-10-02 01:00 MDT** emails, so its detail sections (stack, deals, rent, concessions, arrears, parking, work orders, funnel) are about a day newer than the 10-01 zips. Many differences below are just that day of movement (a suite moving from available to in progress, a work order's days open going up by one, a resident paying).
- The feed's `history` list has only two snapshots (2026-09-30 and 2026-10-01). The 2026-10-01 snapshot comes from the 10-02 emails, which I don't have as files, so it can't be checked against a zip. I compared the zips with the **2026-09-30** snapshot instead.

## Things that need your decision

1. **Faculty47 work order 17445203 vendor.** The report reads "Dependable Appliances (Kingston)"; the feed has "Dependable Appliances". Which should the feed carry?
2. **Faculty47 `inventory` "available on".** The feed leaves it blank for all 19 suites; the Availability report has a date for every suite. Intended, or should the parser fill it?
3. **`deals` has no written rule.** The Grove feed lists 14 suites (the in-progress ones); Faculty47 lists its 2 move-ins. `daily-job-rules.md` only says "{unit, plan, rent, movein} from Rent Roll". The parsers read every future resident (31 rows at the Grove) but the comparison only checks the deals the feed already has. Please say which future residents belong in `deals`.
4. **Faculty47 arrears `due`.** On the 10-01 export, current residents' 0 to 30 day balances add to $15,675 (Smith $3,750, Yhap $4,755, Nichols $4,295, Palekar $1,505, Karch $1,220, Scott $150). The 10-02 feed says $10,700 and shows Smith at $1,500, with Palekar and Karch gone. Probably payments overnight, but I can't see the 10-02 PDF to confirm.
5. **2026-09-30 Faculty47 Work Order Details holds only completed orders.** All 13 rows are "Completed"; none of the open orders are in it, and the feed's 09-30 snapshot says 9 open. The parser drops completed orders (`derive.open_work_orders`), so that export gives 0 open. Was the report run with a status filter that evening?
6. **History snapshots can't be rebuilt from either export.** For the Grove's 2026-09-30 snapshot the feed says leased 14, in progress 16, available 49. The 10-01 export gives 16, 14 and 51; the 09-30 18:13 export gives 12, 19 and 48. `holds` has no rule I can apply, so it isn't compared at all.
7. **Faculty47 Concessions is not in the 2026-10-01 zip** (it is in the 09-30 zip). Per the rules the last figures stay and a "missing" flag is raised. The feed's $65,060 is September's report plus the new 202 lease.
8. **Expiring Leases row layout is untested.** Both 10-01 reports say "returned no data" and the 09-30 zips have no Expiring Leases at all. The parser reads the empty case and returns raw lines for rows, flagged `row_layout_tested: false`. I need one export that actually has expiring leases to finish it.
9. **Month closing.** `derive.budget` treats a month as closed once the export's date is in a later month. On the 09-30 exports September is therefore left out (feed has it), and Faculty47's September NOI on the 09-30 export ($29,435) differs from the re-run on 10-01 ($33,532), which the feed note already explains.
10. **Wording is not compared.** Problem text, work order notes, client-facing notes and prospect note wording are judgment, so only reference numbers, statuses, dates, vendors and amounts are checked.
